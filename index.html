Browser-to-browser file transfer. Files go straight between two browsers. Nothing is uploaded or stored, no account needed.

How it works GitHub
  • Public relays only introduce the two browsers, and they never see the ID. Files never pass through them and are never stored.
  • The connection is WebRTC, without TURN. On a very restrictive network the other person may not be able to connect.
  • Nothing remains after you leave or close the tab. A channel also closes itself after 60 minutes.
